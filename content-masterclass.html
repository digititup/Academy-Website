The definitive blueprint to research, script, produce, and edit high-impact educational video content that commands attention and drives meaningful change.

📚 Curriculum Outline

What You Will Learn (Module by Module)

The step-by-step masterclass curriculum engineered to take you from a raw idea to viral, highly credible explainer videos.

Module 01 Foundation & Mindset

The Philosophy of High-Retention Video

Why 90% of creators fail to hold attention and how to structure content around clarity, objectivity, and intellectual curiosity.

  • The Hook-Insight-Payoff Framework
  • Eliminating bias and finding verified primary sources
  • Designing for high average view duration (AVD)
  • Case study: Dissecting a 1M+ view explainer
Module 02 Deep Research

Investigative Research & Data Sourcing

How to uncover verified government data, financial spreadsheets, parliamentary reports, and global journals without getting misled.

  • The 3-layer verification system
  • Accessing legal & economic public databases in Nepal
  • Fact-checking with counter-arguments
  • Open-sourcing your references for total transparency
Module 03 Scriptwriting

Scriptwriting for Maximum Retention

Writing for the ear, not the eye. How to turn dry statistics and complex policies into cinematic, thrilling scripts.

  • The first 15 seconds formula
  • Open loops and narrative breadcrumbs
  • Visual scripting: Writing sound effects and B-roll cues
  • Nepali script rhythm and colloquial translation
Module 04 Production

Studio Production, Lighting & On-Camera Presence

Getting cinema-grade footage on any budget. Camera settings, 3-point lighting setups, crisp audio engineering, and teleprompter mastery.

  • Acoustic treatment and microphone choices
  • Camera lenses, framing, and eye-level psychology
  • Body language, vocal modulation, and delivery cadence
  • DigitItUp studio setup walkthrough
Module 05 Post-Production

The Retention Editing Architecture

Motion graphics, 3D maps, typography callouts, and audio mixing that prevents the viewer from ever clicking away.

  • Sound design as the hidden glue (Whooshes, risers, impacts)
  • Data visualization: Creating dynamic maps & charts
  • Color grading for cinematic documentary mood
  • Workflow hacks in Premiere Pro & DaVinci Resolve
Module 06 Distribution & Scale

Packaging, Algorithm Mastery & Monetization

Title psychology, CTR-optimizing thumbnail art, YouTube metadata, brand sponsorship deals, and community building.

  • Thumbnail A/B testing and curiosity gaps
  • Sponsorship negotiation: Ethics & brand pitching
  • Repurposing for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts
  • Building DigitItUp Agency level production pipelines
